# Practise rescues.
## Don't leave your friends hanging.

The Slack Rescue Competition rewards slackliners who regularly practise rescues. Run a practice rescue, take a photo as proof, and submit it with everyone involved.

Every rescue counts towards the ranking of everyone who took part.

Practising a rescue exposes you to danger, focus on minimizing risk over speed; it's not a race.
