# Rules

- Each rescue practice counts towards the competition, regardless of its outcome. The goal is to build and maintain expertise through continuous practice.
- The practice line must be rigged to the utmost safety standards.
- Four eyes policy. The "victim" acts as a safety, to double check everything.
- The organizers reserve the right to contact the local community to verify the validity of the submission.

## Roles

- **Victim**: the person being rescued on the line.
- **On-the-line rescuer**: goes out on the line to perform the rescue.
- **Ground rescuer**: Collaborates from the anchor, if required for the specific rescue being practised. *There can be more than one.*

## Submitting a rescue

1. Log in with your ISA account.
2. Upload a photo that clearly shows the rescue happening.
3. Add everyone involved and their role. People who don't have an account on this site yet can be added later by ISA ID or email. The rescue shows up on their profile once they log in.

## Scoring

Each rescue counts one point per role for each person involved.

## Fair play

Admins may remove rescues that don't meet these rules.

By participating in this competition you adhere to the ISA's code of conduct. Misuse of the submissions could lead to a response by the admins, including, but not limited to: banning the account from further participation, getting reported to the ISA code of conduct overseers, withdrawal of ISA account.
